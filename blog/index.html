Age Gap Relationships Blog

Age differences in relationships raise questions that go far beyond arithmetic: how do different cultures view them, what do the statistics actually show, and which legal considerations matter as couples plan their lives together? This blog addresses each of those questions with evidence-based analysis and practical guidance.

The articles below are written for people in age-gap relationships, for their friends and families, and for anyone curious about the research. Every piece links back to the free Age Gap Calculator so you can see the concrete numbers behind the discussion.

A few findings worth knowing up front: most couples in the developed world have a median age gap of around two to five years, roughly eight percent of married couples have a gap of ten years or more, and research consistently finds that communication and aligned values matter more to relationship quality than the age difference itself. The articles below dig into the data, the cultural variation, and the practical considerations behind those numbers.
